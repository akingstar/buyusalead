Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how BuyUsaLead handles information submitted through its website and buyer inquiry form.

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry, we may collect your name, company, business email, phone number, product interest, industry, target geography, estimated monthly volume and message.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review and respond to inquiries, discuss campaign requirements, communicate about requested services and maintain inquiry records.

Form processing and service providers

Our inquiry form is processed using Google Apps Script and Google Sheets. Inquiry notifications may be sent by email to our business contact address. These service providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing

We do not offer submitted inquiry information for sale. We may use service providers to operate the inquiry process, or disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to inquiries, manage business communications and meet applicable obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted, contact Simplequotesolution@zohomail.in.

Children

This website is intended for business users and is not directed to children.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.
